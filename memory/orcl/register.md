## R1 — Reporting perimeter and revenue mechanics

- **R1.1 [FACT]** Oracle has three businesses, each comprising one operating segment: cloud and software (formerly “cloud and license”), hardware, and services. Cloud and software sells enterprise applications and infrastructure through cloud and on-premise deployment models; hardware includes engineered systems, servers, storage, industry-specific hardware, operating systems, virtualization and support; services includes consulting and customer-success services. [S1, Business and Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)
- **R1.2 [FACT]** Oracle Cloud includes Cloud Applications—Fusion applications, NetSuite and industry applications including Oracle Health—and OCI, which includes compute, storage, networking, cloud-native services, AI infrastructure, database and AI services. Software includes license and support revenue for database, middleware, Java, applications and related products. [S1, pp. 3–7 and MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)
- **R1.3 [FACT]** Cloud arrangements generally run one to five years and revenue is recognized ratably or as usage is consumed. Software-license and hardware-product revenue is generally recognized at delivery; software and hardware support is generally recognized ratably over a one-year contract. [S1, MD&A and Note 1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)
- **R1.4 [FACT]** Oracle's segment margin includes revenue less direct segment costs and directly attributable sales and marketing. R&D, G&A, segment stock compensation, acquired-intangible amortization, restructuring, certain allocations, interest, non-operating items and taxes are excluded. Segment margin is therefore not consolidated operating income. Oracle does not track assets by segment. [S1, MD&A and Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)
- **R1.5 [FACT]** Substantially all software-license customers purchase support and substantially all support customers renew annually; support is generally priced as a percentage of net license fees. [S1, Business and MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)

## R1 — Reporting perimeter and products

- **R1.1 [FACT]** NVIDIA has two operating and reportable segments: **Compute & Networking** and **Graphics**. Compute & Networking includes Data Center accelerated-computing and networking platforms, AI solutions and software, and Automotive platforms and software. Graphics includes GeForce gaming/PC GPUs and Quadro/NVIDIA RTX enterprise-workstation graphics. [S2, Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R1.2 [FACT]** Beginning in Q1 FY2027, NVIDIA changed its market-platform presentation to **Data Center** and **Edge Computing** and recast comparable periods. Data Center is split into **Hyperscale** and **AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise (ACIE)**. Edge Computing covers data-processing devices for agentic and physical AI, including PCs, game consoles, workstations, AI-RAN base stations, robotics and automotive. These market platforms are not reportable segments and do not map one-to-one to them; Automotive remains in Compute & Networking. During Q2 FY2027, one company was reclassified from ACIE to Hyperscale and prior-period revenue was recast. [S7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000051/q1fy27pr.htm); [S2, Note 13 and MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R1.3 [FACT]** The FY2026 10-K separately disclosed the older specialized markets—Data Center compute, Data Center networking, Gaming, Professional Visualization, Automotive, and OEM and Other—but that is not the latest quarterly taxonomy. Do not present those six lines as NVIDIA's current reportable segments. [S1, Note 16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm); [S2, Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R1.4 [FACT]** Data Center offerings comprise rack-scale systems, subsystems or modules combining GPUs, CPUs, DPUs, interconnects, networking, systems, software and services. Paid software includes NVIDIA AI Enterprise and vGPU licenses; CUDA and domain libraries form the common development platform. [S1, Business](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
- **R1.5 [FACT]** Blackwell accounted for the majority of system shipments in Q2 FY2027; Vera Rubin began production shipments in Q3 FY2027. NVIDIA said it was shipping both architectures and experiencing supply constraints. [S2, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)

## R5 — Foundry, packaging, memory and capacity

- **R5.1 [FACT]** NVIDIA uses a fabless/contract-manufacturing model. Named wafer foundries are TSMC and Samsung; named memory suppliers are SK hynix, Micron and Samsung; NVIDIA uses CoWoS packaging; named assembly/test/packaging partners include Hon Hai, Wistron and Fabrinet. [S1, Business—Manufacturing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
- **R5.2 [FACT]** Supply commitments were $279 billion at 2026-07-26, up from $119 billion one quarter earlier, for Data Center infrastructure systems and primarily memory and manufacturing facilities. The contractual schedule was $92 billion in the remainder of FY2027, $87 billion in FY2028, $88 billion in FY2029 and $12 billion thereafter. [S2, Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R5.3 [FACT]** NVIDIA said Blackwell and Rubin were facing supply constraints, while customer deployment can also be delayed by land, power, shell, cooling, capital and other Data Center infrastructure. [S2, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R5.4 [FACT]** At 2026-07-26, NVIDIA also had $29 billion of cloud-service commitments, $25 billion of uncommenced Data Center lease commitments, $25 billion of equity-investment commitments and $8 billion of capital-expenditure commitments. These amounts are commitments, not current-period expense or capex. [S2, Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R5.5 [FACT]** NVIDIA had $3.5 billion maximum gross exposure under land/power/shell guarantees for AI-cloud partners. In August 2026 it added phased SB Energy guarantees capped at $105 billion for OpenAI-related leases covering approximately 4.25 GW in Ohio; the first phase was expected in FY2029. The cap is contingent maximum exposure, not a balance-sheet liability or expected cash outflow. [S2, Note 10 and subsequent-event disclosure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R5.6 [FACT]** Foundry allocation, wafer starts, process-node mix, CoWoS capacity/share, HBM generation/volume/content/pricing, production yield and supplier utilization: **not obtained**.

## R8 — Competition, export controls and antitrust

- **R8.1 [FACT]** NVIDIA names AMD, Huawei and Intel among accelerated-computing competitors; Alibaba, Alphabet/Google, Amazon, Baidu, Huawei and Microsoft among cloud companies designing internal AI hardware/software; and AMD, Arista, Broadcom, Cisco, HPE, Huawei, Intel, Lumentum and Marvell among networking competitors. It also cites internal customer silicon as a competitive threat. [S1, Business—Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
- **R8.2 [FACT]** NVIDIA identifies software support, common APIs, breadth/full-stack integration, manufacturing access, price and total system cost as principal competitive factors. CUDA's developer base is an ecosystem asset, but switching cost, software revenue and workload migration cost were not quantified. [S1, Business—Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
- **R8.3 [FACT]** In 1H FY2027 NVIDIA recorded a $0.4 billion H200 excess-inventory/purchase-obligation charge. Licensed H200 shipments to China were less than 1% of Q2 Data Center revenue and carried a 25% U.S. import tariff that NVIDIA said it had not passed through. NVIDIA described itself as effectively foreclosed from China's Data Center compute market under the current U.S./China regime. [S2, MD&A and risk factors](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- **R8.4 [FACT]** NVIDIA has received broad information requests from competition regulators in the EU, U.S., U.K., China and South Korea concerning GPU sales, supply allocation, foundation-model investments/agreements, strategy and customer/supplier arrangements. The filing also describes a French Competition Authority inquiry and China's preliminary Mellanox-related finding. No loss amount or remedy was disclosed. [S2, risk factors](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)

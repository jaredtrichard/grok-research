## R1 — Reporting perimeter and revenue architecture

- **R1.1 [FACT]** Amazon has three reportable segments: North America, International and AWS. North America and International include retail sales of consumer products—including amounts earned from seller activity—plus advertising and subscription services through the applicable stores. AWS consists of global sales of compute, storage, database and other services. There are no internal revenue transactions between reportable segments. [S1, Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)
- **R1.2 [FACT]** Amazon separately discloses net sales groups—Online stores, Physical stores, Third-party seller services, Advertising services, Subscription services, AWS and Other—but these are not seven reportable segments. Advertising and subscriptions are principally embedded in North America and International. [S1, Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/R19.htm)
- **R1.3 [FACT]** Online stores includes product sales and transactional digital content recorded gross. Physical stores includes goods physically selected in-store; online orders for delivery or store pickup remain Online stores. Third-party seller services includes commissions and related fulfillment/shipping fees. Advertising includes sponsored, display and video ads. Subscription services includes Prime fees and non-AWS digital video, audiobook, music, e-book and other subscriptions. Other includes shipping and healthcare services, certain video licensing/distribution and co-branded credit-card agreements. [S3, Note 8, pp. 23–24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000026/amzn-20260630.htm)
- **R1.4 [FACT]** Amazon is principal for merchandise it owns and reports that revenue gross. It is not seller of record for third-party marketplace transactions and recognizes commissions plus related fulfillment/shipping and other seller-service fees. This makes paid-unit mix different from reported-revenue mix. [S1, Item 1 and Note 1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)
- **R1.5 [FACT]** Amazon says operating income is more meaningful than gross profit and gross margin because of its mix of product categories and services. Segment gross profit is not disclosed. [S3, MD&A p. 34](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000026/amzn-20260630.htm)

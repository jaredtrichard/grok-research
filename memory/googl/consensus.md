# Alphabet consensus — what is already priced

As of **2026-09-29**. This home owns consensus framing; actual company results remain in [`register.md`](register.md). Alphabet does **not** publish a Tesla-style company-compiled consensus deck; pre-print third-party estimates below are **secondary** sources, not filings.

## 1. Pre-Q2 2026 earnings expectations (third-party)

Sources: Alphastreet preview citing ~41 analysts ([Q2 2026 preview](https://news.alphastreet.com/alphabet-googl-q2-2026-preview-eps-est-2-89-reports-july-22/), retrieved 2026-09-29); LSEG/StreetAccount figures as summarized in post-earnings media ([Webull recap](https://www.webull.com/blog/294-Alphabet-Q2-2026-Earnings-Revenue-Beats-Cloud-Revenue-Surges-82), citing CNBC/LSEG, July 2026).

| Q2 2026 (consensus before print) | estimate |
|---|---:|
| GAAP / adjusted EPS (Street) | ~$2.89 |
| Total revenue | ~$116.8–117.0bn |
| Google Cloud revenue (subset) | ~$22.4bn (StreetAccount, via media) |

- **[FACT]** Q2 2026 reported consolidated revenue was **$119.8bn** and Cloud revenue **$82%** YoY growth to **$24.8bn** (S3 release; R2).
- **[FACT]** Reported GAAP diluted EPS was **$14.24** for 1H / distorted by **$99.0bn** Q2 equity-security gain (R4.1); recurring operating performance must be read ex-mark, as the model does in `income.md`.
- **[DEDUCTED]** Versus the pre-print revenue consensus, Alphabet beat on revenue; versus ~**$2.89** recurring EPS framing, underlying Q2 EPS (~**$2.85** in third-party adjusted figures) was roughly in line to slightly light—while GAAP headline massively exceeded consensus because of R4.1.
- **[VIEW]** The Street entered Q2 already pricing strong ad and Cloud top-line; the initiation model’s debate is capex, FCF and terminal multiple—not whether Cloud grew fast in Q2.

## 2. Post-Q2 what the tape appears to embed

- **[FACT]** Management raised FY2026 capex guidance to **$195–205bn** (R5.8, S4); Q2 company-defined FCF was **negative $5.9bn** (R5.1).
- **[DEDUCTED]** At **$340.92** (2026-09-29), `valuation.md` implies operating EV **~$3.90tn**, **~17.7×** model FY2028E operating income and **~5.9×** model FY2028E revenue—above the base DCF terminal **20.0×** on OI only after discounting and below a full “AI re-rating” if the tape were capitalizing R4.1 into operating value (it is stripped in this workbook).
- **[FACT]** Post-Q2, as-of-2026-09-29 **official** sell-side compilation of FY2026–FY2028 revenue, EPS, FCF and segment economics: **not obtained**.
- **[FACT]** Post-Q2 analyst price-target distribution from a single primary database: **not obtained**. Secondary media (e.g., Barchart/BofA snippets) cite bullish mean targets in the **$400+** range with wide dispersion; those figures are **not** adopted here because they are not company-compiled and may mix mark-to-market equity gains into forward EPS.

## R1 — Reporting perimeter and revenue architecture

- **R1.1 [FACT]** Alphabet reports Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets. Certain shared AI R&D, corporate initiatives and shared corporate costs are reported in Alphabet-level activities rather than allocated to segments. Revenue hedging gains/losses are also unallocated. Source/as-of: S1, Business and Note 15, 2025-12-31; S2, Note 15, 2026-06-30.
- **R1.2 [FACT]** Google Services includes ads, Android, Chrome, devices, Maps, Play, Search and YouTube. Revenue comes primarily from advertising; consumer subscriptions including YouTube TV, YouTube Music and Premium, NFL Sunday Ticket and Google One; Play apps and in-app purchases; and devices. Source/as-of: S1, Business and Note 15, 2025-12-31.
- **R1.3 [FACT]** Google advertising is disclosed as Google Search & other, YouTube ads, and Google Network. Search & other includes Google search properties, distribution-partner traffic and owned properties including Gmail, Maps and Play. Network includes AdMob, AdSense and Google Ad Manager properties. Source/as-of: S1, MD&A “Google Advertising,” 2025-12-31; S2, Note 1, 2026-06-30.
- **R1.4 [FACT]** Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices combines consumer subscriptions, Play fees, Pixel-family devices, and other products/services; constituent revenue and profit are not separately reported. Source/as-of: S1, Note 1, 2025-12-31; S2, Note 1, 2026-06-30.
- **R1.5 [FACT]** Google Cloud includes consumption and subscriptions for GCP infrastructure/platform services, Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise, cybersecurity, data/analytics, Google Workspace and other enterprise services. Beginning in Q2 2026, it also includes TPU-system product sales. Source/as-of: S2, Note 1 and MD&A, 2026-06-30.
- **R1.6 [FACT]** Other Bets combines operating segments that are individually immaterial; revenue is generated primarily from autonomous transportation and internet services. Source/as-of: S1, Business and Note 15, 2025-12-31; S2, Note 15, 2026-06-30.

- **R6.1 [FACT]** Total revenue backlog was $242,800 million at 2025-12-31, primarily Google Cloud; a precise Cloud-only amount at that date was **not obtained**. Alphabet expected just over 50% of total backlog to be recognized within the next 24 months. Source/as-of: S1 Note 2, 2025-12-31.

## R7 — TAC and content economics

- **R7.1 [FACT]** TAC was $54,900 million in FY2024 and $59,926 million in FY2025. The TAC rate declined from 20.7% to 20.3%, primarily from mix shifting from Network to Search & other; Search and Network TAC rates were substantially consistent year over year. Source/as-of: S1 MD&A, fiscal years ended 2024-12-31 and 2025-12-31.
- **R7.2 [FACT]** TAC was $16,179 million in Q2 2026 and $31,407 million in 1H 2026, versus $14,705 million/$28,453 million in the prior-year periods. The TAC rate declined from 20.6% to 19.8%; Search TAC rate was substantially consistent and Network TAC rate increased slightly. Source/as-of: S2 MD&A, periods ended 2026-06-30.
- **R7.3 [FACT]** TAC includes payments to search distribution partners and to Google Network partners. Other cost of revenue includes YouTube content acquisition, infrastructure depreciation and operations, operations compensation, and device/TPU inventory costs. Source/as-of: S2 Note 1, 2026-06-30.
- **R7.4 [FACT]** Search distribution TAC, Network TAC, YouTube content acquisition cost, and gross profit by Search, YouTube, Network, subscriptions/platforms/devices, Cloud product, or Other Bet are **not obtained** separately. Source/as-of: S1–S2, through 2026-06-30.

## R8 — Competition, regulation and contingencies

- **R8.1 [FACT]** Alphabet identifies competition from general/vertical search and e-commerce, online ad platforms and networks, offline ads, digital-content/app platforms, enterprise cloud, AI model/product providers, hardware/platform companies, digital video, social networks and workspace products. Source/as-of: S1 Business “Competition,” 2025-12-31.
- **R8.2 [FACT]** In the U.S. Search case, the district court ruled against Google in August 2024 and entered final judgment in December 2025. The judgment restricts distribution and requires certain search-data sharing and syndication offers. Google appealed in January 2026; DOJ and state plaintiffs also appealed in February 2026. Source/as-of: S2 Note 10, 2026-06-30.
- **R8.3 [FACT]** In the U.S. DOJ ad-tech case, an April 2025 mixed ruling found neither advertiser tools nor the DoubleClick/AdMeld acquisitions anticompetitive but found publisher tools unfairly excluded rivals. Remedies proceedings included a DOJ structural-remedy proposal; at 2026-06-30 final judgment was pending and a possible loss could not be estimated. Source/as-of: S2 Note 10, 2026-06-30.
- **R8.4 [FACT]** The EC’s September 2025 ad-tech decision imposed a €3.0 billion fine and cease-and-desist direction over alleged self-preferencing. Alphabet recognized a $3,500 million charge in Q3 2025, provided bank guarantees, and appealed in November 2025; the appeal remained pending at 2026-06-30. Source/as-of: S1/S2 Note 10, 2026-06-30.
- **R8.5 [FACT]** The EC Android fine became final in July 2026 after the European Court of Justice denied Alphabet’s appeal; Alphabet paid $5,200 million including accrued interest in July 2026. Source/as-of: S2 Note 10/MD&A, July 2026.
- **R8.6 [FACT]** In Epic v. Google, Google implemented the October 2024 Play remedies in October 2025. After a March 2026 settlement and later withdrawal of a joint motion to modify the injunction, Google remained subject to the remedies at 2026-06-30. Source/as-of: S2 Note 10, 2026-06-30.
- **R8.7 [FACT]** EU DMA investigations into Google Play and Search remained open after March 2025 preliminary non-compliance findings; a probable loss was not reasonably estimable. Source/as-of: S2 Note 10, 2026-06-30.
- **R8.8 [FACT]** The Q2 filing carried $17,356 million of accrued fines and settlements at 2026-06-30. It also recognized $2,100 million related to a PriceRunner decision in Q2 2026, comprising $1,500 million principal damages in Services G&A plus $581 million accrued interest/costs in other income/expense; Alphabet appealed. Source/as-of: S2 Note 10 and MD&A, 2026-06-30.
- **R8.9 [FACT]** Alphabet states that privacy/data/AI rules can require notices, consents, opt-outs, data sharing/portability and Android interoperability, and can lead to fines or product/practice changes. Source/as-of: S2 Risk Factors, 2026-06-30.

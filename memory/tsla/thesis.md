# Tesla working thesis

GF-TSLA-1 · as of 2026-08-29 · name id `tsla`

Numbers live in the workbook. This file owns the claim, the mechanism, and the killing conditions. Official 12-month price target: [`models/tsla/valuation.md`](../../models/tsla/valuation.md). Do not treat a restated figure here as a second target.

## Claim

The statements Tesla files — Automotive (vehicles, leasing, credits), Energy generation and storage, and Services and other — plus the researcher [VIEW] path in `models/tsla/`, capitalize to the official 12-month PT in `valuation.md`. Last close $348.75 (2026-08-28) leaves the residual shown in that file as value that is not in those statements. Robotaxi, incremental FSD, and Optimus are $0 in the base forecast because standalone economics are `not obtained` (R8.3). If the model does not say it, it is not this thesis.

## Why this view is right

1. Auto-ex-services revenue fell FY2023–FY2025 (R2) while deliveries also fell (R3). The forecast only restores volume to 1.72 / 1.85 / 2.00 million on a held FY2025 realized-revenue quotient, not a new ASP.
2. Q2 2026 volume was strong and Energy grew, but automotive ex-credit GM was 16.3%, Energy GM 20.4%, company OI $398m, and FCF −$1,092m with FY2026 capex guided above $25bn (R2, R5.8). The model does not snap margins back to FY2023–FY2024.
3. Company opex is modeled at $17.0 / $18.2 / $19.2bn against GP $17.6 / $20.1 / $22.8bn, so FY2026–FY2027 EBIT stays thin. That is the AI/R&D and SG&A path, not a temporary one-quarter spike.
4. Credits are a declining standalone line (R7.3), not vehicle ASP.
5. 20× FY2027E EBIT is already a premium to the Toyota/BYD EBIT framing in the comps table. A software multiple would be a claim the income statement does not support.

## What others miss or get wrong

1. Pre-Q2 company-compiled FY2026 GAAP EPS averaged $1.25 with 1.73 million deliveries (`consensus.md`). The model’s FY2026 EPS is $0.35 on 1.72 million deliveries. The gap is margin and opex, not volume.
2. Delivery consensus already assumed growth through FY2028. A bull case that is only “more cars” is largely in the June/July compilation.
3. Treating the last price as a Robotaxi or Optimus appraisal is a category error until those lines exist. The residual in `valuation.md` is arithmetic, not a fleet or take-rate model.
4. Post-Q2, as-of-2026-08-29 Street PTs and refreshed EPS: `not obtained`. Do not pretend the July 17 compilation is still the tape.

## Mechanism and magnitude

- Mechanism: GP grows with deliveries, Energy GWh, and Services; opex and capex grow with AI/manufacturing spend; credits fade; autonomy P&L stays at zero; share count is the 3,949,547,394 filing figure.
- Magnitude: see official PT and last-price residual in `valuation.md`. Model FY2026–28 FCF is negative. Current operating EV / FY2028E EBIT is the multiple in that file’s market-implied table; the official method uses 20× FY2027E EBIT.

# Tesla consensus — what is already priced

As of 2026-08-29. This home owns consensus figures; actual company results remain in [`register.md`](register.md). Tesla states that company-compiled consensus does not constitute its endorsement of analyst information, recommendations or conclusions.

- **[FACT]** Median total-delivery estimates were 408,609 for Q2 2026 and 1,667,842 for FY2026.
- **[FACT]** This June 26 delivery snapshot was superseded for FY2026 by the later July 17 earnings compilation below.
- **[DEDUCTED]** The pre-release Street frame already assumed sustained Energy deployment growth and delivery growth beyond FY2026; a flat-volume/no-growth setup was not the consensus.
- **[DEDUCTED]** Q2 actual delivery volume materially exceeded the pre-release average, while Energy deployment was slightly below it. See R3 for actuals; the comparison does not establish the durability or profitability of the volume.

## 2. Company-compiled earnings consensus

Source: Tesla IR, [Q2 2026 earnings consensus](https://ir.tesla.com/press-release/earnings-consensus-second-quarter-2026), published 2026-07-17; average of the contributing sell-side estimates.

| Q2 2026 **[FACT]** | consensus |
|---|---:|
| Automotive revenue | $20.05bn |
| Energy generation and storage revenue | $3.77bn |
| Services and other revenue | $3.76bn |
| Total revenue | $27.584bn |
| GAAP gross margin | 19.5% |
| GAAP operating margin | 5.4% |
| GAAP diluted EPS | $0.36 |
| Non-GAAP diluted EPS | $0.55 |
| Capital expenditures | $6.70bn |
| Free cash flow | $(3.25)bn |

- **[FACT]** The same compilation’s FY2026 averages included $105.221bn total revenue, $1.25 GAAP diluted EPS, $2.08 non-GAAP diluted EPS and 1,728,925 deliveries.
- **[DEDUCTED]** Against this pre-report frame, Q2 revenue and free cash flow were better than consensus, while gross margin, operating margin and EPS were worse. Actuals are not repeated here; see R2 and R5.
- **[DEDUCTED]** The July 17 FY2026 delivery average was above the June 26 average, demonstrating that consensus had moved before earnings rather than remaining at the earlier delivery snapshot.
- **[VIEW]** The miss pattern matters more than the top-line beat for the initiation model: the pre-report consensus had not priced the full near-term operating-cost and gross-margin burden visible in Q2, while its delivery path already embedded growth.

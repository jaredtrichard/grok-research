## R1 — Reporting perimeter and products

- **R1.1 [FACT]** Tesla has two operating and reportable segments: (i) Automotive and (ii) Energy generation and storage. Automotive includes vehicle design, development, manufacturing, sales and leasing, regulatory credits, and “Services and other.” Services and other includes used vehicles, non-warranty maintenance and collision, paid Supercharging, automotive insurance, parts and merchandise. Energy includes generation/storage products, leasing, financing, related services and incentives. [S1, p. 2 and Note 16, p. 91](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- **R1.2 [FACT]** Automotive, Energy generation and storage, and Services and other are separately presented revenue/cost lines, but Services and other is not a separate reportable segment. FSD (Supervised), Robotaxi, insurance and Supercharging are not separately reported financial segments or revenue lines. [S1, pp. 46, 48, 91](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- **R1.3 [FACT]** Tesla’s consumer vehicles at FY2025 were Model 3, Y, S, X and Cybertruck; it also had early Tesla Semi production/deliveries. Robotaxi launched in June 2025 using Model Y; Cybercab is the purpose-built planned fleet vehicle. FSD offered in customer vehicles remained supervised. [S1, pp. 2–3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- **R1.4 [FACT]** Powerwall serves homes/small commercial sites; Megapack serves commercial, industrial, utility and generation customers. Tesla also identifies Autobidder and Powerhub as energy-control software. [S1, p. 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)

## R7 — Competition, supply and regulation

- **R7.1 [FACT]** Tesla sources from thousands of suppliers; some components are single-source. Named inputs include battery cells, electronics, complex assemblies, aluminum, steel, lithium, nickel and copper. Tesla cited qualification of alternate suppliers, safety stock, long-term contracts and localization/vertical integration as mitigants. [S1, pp. 7, 16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- **R7.2 [FACT]** Tesla described automotive competition as established/new EV makers plus internal-combustion vehicles, autonomous-service competition as ride-hailing/taxi and AI companies, and energy competition as storage manufacturers, integrators and conventional generators. [S1, pp. 10–11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- **R7.3 [FACT]** Tesla said recent governmental actions restricted some regulatory-credit programs; credit revenue depends on Tesla’s credit supply, regulation and other automakers’ demand. [S3, pp. 11, 31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026049270/tsla-20260630.htm)
- **R7.4 [FACT]** Tesla’s 10-K identifies NHTSA authority and a U.S. state/city regulatory patchwork for autonomous vehicles; ECE/EU design, AI, data and marketing restrictions; and evolving, potentially different China autonomy rules. [S1, pp. 8–9](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
- **R7.5 [FACT]** Tesla said tariffs and trade/fiscal policy can affect components, demand and profitability, with relatively greater tariff impact on Energy than Automotive. Following a February 2026 U.S. Supreme Court ruling invalidating certain IEEPA tariffs, Tesla recorded no refund receivable because recoverability/timing remained uncertain. [S3, pp. 23, 28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026049270/tsla-20260630.htm)
- **R7.6 [FACT]** The Q2 10-Q disclosed ongoing regulator information requests, including from NHTSA, on vehicle functionality, incidents, Autopilot, FSD and Robotaxi; Tesla stated no agency in an ongoing investigation had concluded wrongdoing to its knowledge. [S3, p. 25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026049270/tsla-20260630.htm)

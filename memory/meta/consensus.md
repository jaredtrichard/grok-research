# Meta Platforms consensus — what is already priced

As of 2026-09-29. This home owns the external narrative; company facts remain in [`register.md`](register.md). No recommendation or rating is assigned.

## 1. The official operating bar

- **[FACT]** Meta's Q2 2026 outlook called for Q3 revenue of 61,000–64,000, FY2026 total expenses of 165,000–169,000, FY2026 capex including finance-lease principal of 130,000–145,000, and FY2026 operating income above FY2025. The company also expected FY2026 RL operating loss similar to FY2025. [Q2 2026 official release](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/)
- **[DEDUCTED]** The published bar already combines strong revenue with a step-up in AI infrastructure and R&D. A thesis based only on "AI raises costs" is not differentiated.
- **[DEDUCTED]** The RL loss burden is also explicit. Treating a large FY2026 RL loss as a surprise would misstate what management had already framed.

## 2. Sell-side / market narrative

- **[DEDUCTED]** The constructive narrative is that recommendation models improve engagement while automated targeting, measurement, creative, and campaign tools improve advertiser returns. That allows impression and price growth to coexist even with slower user growth.
- **[DEDUCTED]** The skeptical narrative is that infrastructure spending, depreciation, technical compensation, and legal/regulatory costs arrive before durable proof of incremental AI revenue and can compress free cash flow.
- **[DEDUCTED]** WhatsApp paid messaging and subscriptions are generally treated as additional monetization vectors, but the absence of disclosed volume, ARPU, and standalone margin prevents a precise primary-source consensus bridge.
- **[DEDUCTED]** Reality Labs is framed as an expensive option on AI glasses and a future computing platform. The disclosed loss is visible; the debate is the duration and terminal value of that spending, not whether the current segment is profitable.
- **[DEDUCTED]** The key valuation debate is therefore FoA durability and cash conversion after AI capex, with RL as a separately visible drag. The tape need not assume near-term RL breakeven.

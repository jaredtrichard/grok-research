# Meta Platforms working thesis

GF-META-1 · as of 2026-09-29 · name id `meta`

Numbers live in the workbook. This file owns the claim, mechanism, and killing conditions. Official 12-month price target: [`models/meta/valuation.md`](../../models/meta/valuation.md). Do not treat any restatement here as a second target.

## Claim

**[VIEW]** FoA can absorb the FY2026 AI-infrastructure, R&D, legal, and severance burden and rebuild operating leverage through FY2027E because AI improves both sides of the ad engine: recommendation supports engagement/impressions, while targeting, measurement, and creative tools support advertiser returns and price. The official value applies a mature but premium digital-platform multiple to combined FoA plus RL operating income; it does not require RL breakeven or add a separate RL option value.

The investment idea is therefore a cash-conversion and operating-leverage claim, not “AI” as an unmodeled narrative. See the segment path in [`segments.md`](../../models/meta/segments.md), the expense and profit bridge in [`income.md`](../../models/meta/income.md), and the official method in [`valuation.md`](../../models/meta/valuation.md).

## Why this view is right

1. **[FACT] The ad engine has two disclosed growth legs.** FY2025 and Q2 2026 both showed positive impression and price growth (R3). **[VIEW]** The model decelerates advertising growth rather than extending the latest rate unchanged, while preserving a two-sided contribution from engagement/inventory and advertiser demand.
2. **[VIEW] The FY2026 margin trough is explicit.** FoA operating margin falls in FY2026E before recovering in FY2027E–FY2028E. R&D and D&A rise materially, and FY2026E company free cash flow is negative in [`cashflow.md`](../../models/meta/cashflow.md). The thesis therefore does not hide the infrastructure bill.
3. **[FACT] RL is already separately visible.** Its disclosed operating loss sits below FoA in the combined segment build (R2). **[VIEW]** The model carries a large loss through FY2028E and assigns no separate value to undisclosed hardware/software optionality.
4. **[VIEW] FoA Other helps but does not carry the case.** Paid messaging and subscriptions grow faster from a small base; the official value still depends principally on advertising and FoA operating margin.
5. **[VIEW] The official multiple is the judgment.** It is applied to FY2027E combined operating income after the RL loss. The much lower DCF check in `valuation.md` makes the capital-intensity risk visible instead of burying it.

## What others may miss

1. **[DEDUCTED]** The expense and capex step-up is already in management's public bar (`consensus.md`). Merely identifying higher AI spending is not differentiated; the debate is whether recommendation and ad-tool gains outrun the D&A and R&D follow-through.
2. **[DEDUCTED]** Impression growth and price growth are not interchangeable. Lower-monetizing geography and Reels mix can raise impressions while diluting price, so both drivers must remain visible (R3).
3. **[DEDUCTED]** RL does not need an invented sum-of-parts value to matter. Its loss already reduces company EBIT and therefore the official equity value.
4. **[DEDUCTED]** SBC is a real cash/shareholder variable even when added back in operating cash flow. The balance and cash-flow model carries SBC, net-share-settlement taxes, and a stable diluted denominator explicitly.
5. **[FACT]** A refreshed post-Q2 primary-source Street estimate set was not obtained (R8.5). This thesis does not claim a precise consensus earnings delta.

## Mechanism and magnitude

- **[VIEW] Mechanism:** Advertising plus FoA Other produces FoA revenue; FoA margin produces FoA operating income; RL operating loss is then combined with FoA to produce company EBIT. Functional expenses reconcile to the identical EBIT with no corporate profit plug.
- **[VIEW] Cash conversion:** net income, D&A, SBC, and core working capital produce OCF; property purchases and finance-lease principal produce FCF. Marketable securities, debt, dividends, repurchases, and share-settlement taxes complete ending cash.
- **[VIEW] Valuation:** the official bridge uses FY2027E combined operating income and FY2027E net cash. Bear, bull, and DCF checks are sensitivities, not alternative official targets. See `valuation.md`.

## Killing conditions

| kill | status | what would break the modeled claim | when to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising engine misses both legs **[VIEW]** | Open | reported impression and price growth together fall materially below the model's revenue path, without a compensating disclosed driver | Q3/Q4 2026 releases and 10-K |
| FoA leverage fails **[VIEW]** | Open | FoA operating margin does not recover toward the FY2027E path after severance/legal effects, or infrastructure cost rises faster than monetization | each quarterly segment table |
| Capex does not crest **[VIEW]** | Open | 2027–2028 capex stays near or above the FY2026 build while operating cash flow does not scale, invalidating the cash-conversion path | FY2026 10-K outlook; 2027 quarterly cash flow |
| RL loss expands beyond the carried path **[VIEW]** | Open | annual RL loss moves materially above the model without a corresponding revenue/strategic milestone | quarterly segment note and annual outlook |
| FoA Other evidence contradicts the path **[VIEW]** | Open | paid messaging/subscription disclosure shows weak adoption, price, or retention relative to the modeled aggregate growth | earnings transcript and any KPI disclosure |
| Shareholder denominator rises **[VIEW]** | Open | dilution and net-share settlement exceed the stable diluted-share path while buybacks remain constrained | quarterly EPS/share and equity tables |
| Valuation regime de-rates **[VIEW]** | Open | durable evidence supports a multiple materially below the official method because AI returns remain below cost of capital | every model refresh; do not react to price alone |

A price move alone does not kill the thesis. A narrative claim without a disclosed KPI does not replace a model input. If new primary disclosure supplies product economics currently marked `not obtained`, replace the `[VIEW]`; do not layer it on top.

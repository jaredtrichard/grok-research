# EQX house model (md+csv)

- Clock: **3y run-rate = 2026 printed guide** (12 months EQX + Aug–Dec Musselwhite / Camino Rojo). Not a 2029 volume model.
- Units: US$m unless noted. Gold US$/oz. Volume koz attributable.
- Official PT: **EV / guide-built EBITDA × this name’s sourced 2019–2025 k range** (base = mid of range **9.16**, bull = high **10.90**, bear = low **7.41**). See `hist_k.csv`.
- **7.0× T. Rowe is a check only.** FCF yield is a check. Do not average.
- Official mid = company guide. Miss lives in the view node, not a second mid.
- Every material line is sourced or labelled computed. Missing = UNSOURCED, not a plug.
- IS/BS/CF **tie** (`tie.csv`): BS.cash = CF.ending_cash; RE = open RE + NI − div; assets = L+E.
- EV plug is **30 Jun net debt $96.22m** (debt ex-convert $414 − cash $317.78). Run-rate ending cash is the CF/BS tie, **not** the EV plug.
- Converts ITM → dilution in N=1,247,524,702.

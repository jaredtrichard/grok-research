## What is not comparable

These are definition / perimeter gaps already flagged in the scouts. They are why this table is a **hand-off**, not a ranking.

1. **GEO vs gold koz.** FNV headlines **GEOs** (Au + Ag + PGM + iron/energy at **fixed 2026** ratios). KGC headlines **attrib. GEO** (silver at period spot ratio; Q2 **61.61:1**). NEM/B/AEM/AGI/EQX/BTG gold ounces are **not** GEOs. Never drop FNV (or KGC GEO) into a koz column without the label.
2. **AISC definitions are not one function.** NEM headlines **by-product** AISC (co-product is higher and is what the 10-Q “Gold AISC” line is). Barrick is WGC-style attrib. AISC with copper stripped. AEM is by-product **per oz produced**, composition changed 1 Jan 2026. KGC is attrib. AISC **per GEO sold**. AGI is per oz **sold**, company-defined. EQX AISC is **internal**, **excludes corp G&A**, excludes Castle Mountain / Los Filos from the consol. unit-cost set. BTG AISC includes royalties/production taxes (Fekola Q2 royalties+tax **$90.4m** vs production costs **$129.2m**). **FNV has no miner AISC.**
3. **FNV vs miners.** Royalty/stream cash cost/GEO is delivery + a little tax/energy opex, not a mine AISC. Profit-based slice (H1 **12%** of FNV revenue) *does* see operator costs. Do not put $347/GEO next to $1,459–$2,175 miner AISC as a curve.
4. **EQX consolidation.** Q2/H1 prints are **pre-Orla**. 2026 guide is **12+5 months**, not a 12-month combined run-rate. Calibre closed **17 Jun 2025**; Orla **31 Jul 2026**. Deck “543,935 oz combined YTD” is **marketing add of two legal entities, not IFRS**. Do not treat stub prints as senior run-rate.
5. **By-product credits at this tape.** High gold **and** high copper/silver inflate by-product credits (NEM Cu/Ag/Pb/Zn; Barrick Cu AISC $3.95/lb Q2; KGC/AEM/AGI silver credits; BTG silver small). NEM Q1 vs Q2 by-product AISC jump ($1,029 → $1,621) already shows credit/volume sensitivity. Co-product vs by-product can move NEM by **~$300/oz** in the same quarter.
6. **Attributable vs 100%.** NEM NGM is **38.5%** (Q2 240 koz). Barrick NGM is **61.5%** (Q2 382 koz); 100% NGM **623 koz** is in B mine-stats. BTG guide and Q2 table are **100% operated** (Fekola 80%, Otjikoto 90%, Regional live **65%**). KGC attrib. = 70% Manh Choh. AEM/AGI/EQX (post-close) print 100% of named mines. FNV is **interest %**, not operator ounces.
7. **Reserve gold-price assumptions differ (YE 2025 unless noted).** NEM-operated **$2,000** (NGM/PV operator **$1,500**). Barrick **$1,500**. AEM base **$1,600** (site exceptions $1,300–$2,000). KGC **$2,000**. AGI **$1,800** (PDA $1,600). EQX **mixed** $1,350–$2,500 and **pre-Orla** (Orla P&P 3,728 koz quoted from Orla AIF, not a post-close EQX R&R). BTG **$1,750–$2,000**. Do not NAV any book at Kitco $4,602.
8. **FCF definitions differ.** NEM/AEM/AGI/BTG company FCF deducts **growth** capex (AGI also **adds back** hedge buyouts). B/KGC “attrib. FCF” deducts **all** attrib. capex. EQX mine-site FCF nets **non-sustaining at sites**. FNV **does not print FCF**. Desk after-sustaining lines in B/KGC/AEM/AGI scouts are **labelled inferences**.
9. **Guidance price decks differ.** NEM/B/AEM/KGC cost guides at **$4,500/oz**. BTG at **$5,000/oz**. FNV GEO ruler **$4,500**. AGI Mexico cash-tax budget **$4,000**. EQX updated-guide **gold-price assumption: not in scout**. Royalties/AISC move with the deck; a common-price restatement does not exist in these files.
10. **NGM / $1.95bn is a paired NEM–B event only.** Locked: **61.5/38.5 not restated**; **$1.95bn cash after Fourmile** (8-K); deal **not closed as of 17 Aug**; **YE 2026 IPO is Barrick colour**. Two **$1.95bn** figures (cash NEM→Barrick Member vs deemed NEM contrib into NGM). A third lookalike is NEM **2026 sustaining-capex guide $1,950m** — **not the same cash**.

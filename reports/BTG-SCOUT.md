## 5. Costs

**AISC footnote (company, Q2 PR Non-IFRS):** B2Gold defines AISC per ounce as post-commercial cash operating costs + royalties and production taxes + sustaining capex and sustaining exploration + sustaining leases + corporate G&A + RSU/DSU/PSU share-based pay + community relations + reclamation accretion + realized (gains)/losses on **fuel** derivatives, divided by post-commercial gold ounces **sold**.

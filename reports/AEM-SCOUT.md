**Footnote — AEM AISC / TCC (company-defined, not WGC-standardized here):**
- Both are **non-GAAP**, **per ounce of gold produced**, and unless stated otherwise **by-product**.
- **TCC (by-product):** production costs ± inventory / PPA inventory ± realized cost-hedge P&L + in-kind royalties + smelting/refining/marketing − by-product metal impact, ÷ payable gold oz produced. From **1 Jan 2026**, also **adjust for the NTI Payment** at Amaruq (so Meadowbank / Nunavut / consolidated only). NTI is a net-profits royalty to Nunavut Tunngavik Inc.; AEM treats the **Crown** Nunavut NPR as an **income tax** and therefore **excludes** it from TCC/AISC — the 2026 change is to treat NTI similarly for comparability.
- **AISC (IR glossary + Q2 note):** aggregate of **TCC (by-product) + sustaining capex (including sustaining capitalized exploration) + G&A (including stock-option expense) + reclamation expenses**, ÷ gold oz produced. Co-product AISC is the same without the by-product credit.
- **Not in TCC/AISC:** income taxes, interest, dividends. Cash taxes are guided separately (**$3.4–3.6bn** in 2026). NTI cash is guided **$185–195m** at **$4,500/oz** and is **in production costs / out of TCC**.
- Composition **changed 1 Jan 2026**. Do not mix 2025-as-originally-printed with 2026.

**Site TCC outliers (Q2):** Detour **$825** (portfolio low); Pinos Altos **$1,873** by-product / **$2,798** co-product (silver credit is large). CM Q2 TCC **$1,185**; FY CM TCC now guided **~$1,260** vs **$1,187** pre-Barnat.

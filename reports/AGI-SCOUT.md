Guide AISC is **per ounce of gold sold**, company-defined. Consolidated AISC includes corporate & admin and share-based compensation. Mine-site AISC allocates a slice of SBC but **not** corporate G&A.

**AISC / TCC definition footnote (company, Q2 NR / R&R cautionary / MD&A non-GAAP):**
1. **Total cash costs per ounce of gold sold** — mining and processing costs allocated from in-process and doré inventory **plus associated royalties** on ounces of gold sold. **Exclusive of exploration.** Non-GAAP; no IFRS standard meaning.
2. **All-in sustaining costs (AISC) per ounce of gold sold** — total cash costs **plus** exploration, corporate and administrative, share-based compensation, **and sustaining capital** (and, at IGD, sustaining finance leases in the FCF build). This is **what Alamos headlines**.
3. **Mine-site AISC** — TCC + site exploration + site sustaining capital; **no** corporate G&A allocation (SBC partially allocated).
4. **Sustaining capital** — expenditures that do **not** increase annual gold ounce production at a mine site and **exclude** all development-project spend.
5. **Growth / non-sustaining capital** — Phase 3+, IGD Expansion, PDA, Lynn Lake, and other expansion. Called out separately; **not** in AISC.
6. WGC-style AISC and peer AISC **are not the same function**. Do not rank Alamos on this print vs seniors.

Cost pressure colour in the Q2 body: Canadian labour/contractor inflation, diesel/energy, YD grade/rate, LYG stack/grade. Partial offset: weaker CAD. Earnings-call colour on a mid-year Canadian retention program (~$30/oz) is **call, not MD&A table** — treat as colour.
